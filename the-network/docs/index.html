Production management for sports broadcasting

The Network is an internal platform that helps sports broadcasting teams manage shows, videos, clips, scheduling, and platform analytics — all in one place.

A desktop application for Windows & macOS

Show & Video Management

Create shows, track video production stages from filming through to upload, and manage the full episode workflow.

Platform Analytics

Connect YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, and Spotify to view unified analytics — views, watch time, engagement — across all channels.

Production Scheduling

Plan recording sessions, schedule uploads, and coordinate team availability through an integrated calendar.

Team Management

Manage roles, positions, approvals, and communications for the full production team across departments.

Footage & Asset Tracking

Track raw footage, graphics, edits, and exports with automatic folder structure and file monitoring.

Internal Use Only

The Network is a private internal tool used exclusively by authorised production staff.
